## 🔍 Detailed Metric Analysis

### 1. Page Availability & Reach
- **Before**: Only core e-commerce pages were available. Significant user concerns (Login, Validity, Policy) were handled via generic layouts or hidden in raw text.
- **After**: **21+ high-priority routes** are now live. Discovery of every blog post and FAQ category is guaranteed via the automated structural map.

### 2. Google Ranking Potential (Keyword Power)
- **Before**: Lacked a content strategy. The site was "functional" but missed "informational" search traffic.
- **After**: 
    - **Blog Hub**: Targets high-volume, long-tail keywords related to "gift voucher tips" and "brand sales".
    - **FAQ Hub**: Targets "how-to" queries that Google loves to feature in "People Also Ask" snippets.

### 3. Indexing Automation
- **Before**: Human interaction was required to update sitemaps. Risked 404s or outdated links in search results.
- **After**: **Zero-touch automation**. The site indexes itself during the build process, ensuring Google always has the most current structural map immediately.

### 4. Mobile experience Benchmarks
- **Before**: Standard responsiveness. Some padding and button sizes were suboptimal for mobile-first users.
- **After**: **Super Mobile-Friendly**. All help and editorial pages are precision-tuned for small screens, ensuring zero layout shift and high interaction scores (Core Web Vitals).
